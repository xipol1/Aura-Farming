# Creator Outreach

How to find and pitch micro-influencers without paying them.

---

## Target profile

| Filter | Value |
|---|---|
| Follower range | 5,000 – 50,000 |
| Engagement rate | > 3% |
| Niche | Humor / college / Gen Z relatable / Indian meme accounts |
| Geography | India Tier 1 + diaspora |
| Posting frequency | At least 3 Reels/week (active accounts only) |
| Audience age | 18-25 estimated |

**Avoid**: Brand-safe lifestyle, beauty, parenting, fitness. They won't post a sarcastic meme game.

---

## How to find them

1. Search Instagram for hashtags: `#indianmemes`, `#genzindia`, `#delhimemes`, `#mumbaimemes`, `#desimemes`, `#crickettwitter`.
2. Click on top-performing posts (last 7 days).
3. Check the creator's profile. If follower range matches and content niche fits → add to outreach list.
4. Repeat for YouTube Shorts and Twitter.
5. Maintain a spreadsheet: handle, follower count, niche, contact preference (DM/email), date contacted, response.

Target: build a list of 100, contact 25/week.

## The cold DM

### Template A — niche meme account

```
Hey [first name if visible, else "hey"]

Built a thing you might find funny:
auraoff.app

It's a Higher-or-Lower game but for "aura" — cultural moments with satirical scores. India-focused content. Built in 3 weeks, free, ad-free, anonymous.

If it actually makes you laugh, no pressure but a Reel would be sick. Happy to make you a custom branded card if you want one for the post.

Either way, hope you have a decent day.
[your first name]
```

**Why this works**:
- No flattery preamble (instantly identifiable as outreach)
- States the thing, links it, explains in 3 facts
- Explicit no-pressure ask
- Offers a small custom thing (cheap for us)
- Sign off respects their time

### Template B — small creator who's posted gaming or app reviews

```
Hi [name],

Saw your [recent reel topic] — sent it to my [friend/cousin/etc]. 

Made a small thing I think your audience would get:
auraoff.app — Higher-or-Lower for "aura". Skibidi has -41k. Dhoni has 298k. Andrew Tate has -71k.

If it lands for you, no pressure but a Reel format that works well: "I made my [parent/friend] play". The reactions are gold.

Either way, thanks for the [recent reel] vibe.
[your name]
```

**Why this works**:
- One specific genuine line about their work (verifiable, not bullshit)
- Three concrete data points from the deck (creates curiosity)
- Suggests a format that's easy to record
- Doesn't oversell

### Template C — Twitter / X creator

Twitter DMs limit length. Shorter:

```
hey — built a sarcastic Higher-or-Lower game for "aura": auraoff.app

skibidi has -41k aura. dhoni has 298k. andrew tate has -71k.

if you find it funny, a quote tweet would be unreasonably kind. either way, your tweets crack me up.
```

## What we offer

Nothing financial in v1. What we can offer:

1. **Custom share card with their handle** featured (5-minute customization, high perceived value)
2. **Mention in our pinned post / featured row** ("featured by [creator]")
3. **Early access** to themed decks before public launch (when those exist)
4. **A real human relationship** — we reply, we don't ghost, we send updates

What we will not do:
- Pay for posts (we have no budget; setting precedent destroys margins later)
- Pretend a paid post is organic
- Use AI to write personalized messages at scale (it shows)

## Response handling

### If they respond positively but vaguely

```
amazing, thanks for checking it out. if a Reel happens, tag me — i'll boost it and send you the custom card.

no rush, no expectation.
```

### If they ask "what's in it for me"

Be honest:

```
Right now, nothing financial — pre-launch indie project with zero budget. 

What I can offer: custom branded card, mention in our pinned posts, early access to new content. 

Total transparency: I'm trying to find 3-5 creators whose audiences would actually like the game and just let it happen organically. If that's not your style I get it.
```

### If they ghost

That's the default outcome. Move on. Expected response rate: 5-10%. Of those who respond, maybe 30% post. So 25 DMs → 1-2 actual Reels. Volume matters.

### If they post a Reel

1. Like, comment within 30 minutes.
2. Repost the Reel to your Instagram Stories with a thank-you.
3. DM them a quick "thank you, this got X views, here's the custom card you wanted".
4. Don't follow up asking for another post. They earned the right to choose.

## Outreach tracking spreadsheet

Columns:
- Handle
- Platform
- Followers
- Niche
- Engagement rate
- Date contacted
- Template used
- Responded? (yes/no/positive)
- Posted? (yes/no)
- Notes

Update weekly. After 8 weeks, run a retro on which templates and niches converted best.

---

## What not to do

- Mass DM identical message to 100 creators. Instagram flags this fast.
- DM creators outside your follower-range target. Mega-influencers (>100k) won't respond to cold DMs. Don't waste your time.
- Send long pitches. 5 lines max.
- Send a deck or business plan. They don't care.
- Follow up more than once.
- Argue with creators who say no.
- Buy fake engagement to look more legit. Algorithm catches it, and creators check.

## Outreach quota for launch week

- Day 1 (launch): 10 DMs to your warmest 10 contacts (people you've engaged with before)
- Day 2-7: 5 DMs per day to cold contacts from the list (25 total)
- Week 2: 25 more (next batch)
- Week 3+: 25/week steady state

Total target: 100 cold DMs in first 4 weeks → expected 5-10 organic posts.
