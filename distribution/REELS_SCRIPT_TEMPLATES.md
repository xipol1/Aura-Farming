# Reels Script Templates

Five formats. Six Reels per format = 30 total Reels for the launch wave.

Each template gives: hook, structure, props/visuals, sample script, target length.

---

## Format 1 — "Bet I can streak X"

**Length**: 15s
**Why it works**: framing as a challenge invites the viewer to compete. Streak number creates curiosity.

**Structure**:
- 0-2s: hook line, face on camera
- 2-12s: gameplay screen recording with reaction cuts
- 12-14s: the death moment, exaggerated reaction
- 14-15s: CTA

**Sample script**:
> "Bet I can streak 50 in this aura game without dying. [cut to screen] Easy. Easy. Easy. [pause] Wait. WHAT? [death screen] Bro. Skibidi has more aura than that. Link in bio."

**Variations** (record one of each):
1. Bet I can streak 50
2. I'm going for 100 or I quit
3. My friend bet me ₹500 I can't streak 30
4. Highest streak in 2 minutes
5. No looking, just vibes only
6. One-handed run, 50+ or nothing

## Format 2 — "POV: 3am with this game"

**Length**: 12s
**Why it works**: relatability + dark humor. Hits the "I should be sleeping" universal.

**Structure**:
- 0-2s: POV title overlay on a dark room
- 2-10s: looping gameplay with eye-bag filter, lo-fi audio
- 10-12s: cut to clock showing 3:47am

**Sample script** (mostly visual, minimal voice):
> [Title: "POV: it's 3am and you can't stop"]
> [Gameplay sounds only]
> [Cut: clock shows 3:47am]
> [Text: "this game is illegal"]

**Variations**:
1. 3am
2. Avoiding your responsibilities
3. Pretending to study for finals
4. Hiding from your group chat
5. After your situationship ghosted you
6. While your mom thinks you're sleeping

## Format 3 — "I made [person] play"

**Length**: 20s
**Why it works**: introduces a second character. Cross-generational humor is shareable.

**Structure**:
- 0-3s: setup ("I made my X play")
- 3-15s: their reactions to weird comparisons
- 15-18s: their score reveal
- 18-20s: CTA

**Sample script**:
> "I made my mom play Aura Off. She doesn't know what aura is. [hand phone to mom] [mom plays, picks weird things] [moment where she picks a cringe one over a sigma one] [her face] Mom, no. [score reveal: 12 streak] Mom called me an idiot for asking. Link in bio."

**Variations**:
1. My mom
2. My dad
3. My grandma (only if she's cool with it)
4. My best friend
5. My girlfriend/boyfriend
6. My boss (if you're brave)

## Format 4 — "Wait, this is more aura than THAT?"

**Length**: 10s
**Why it works**: shock format. The controversy is the content.

**Structure**:
- 0-1s: screenshot of a controversial pair, mouth-open filter
- 1-8s: explain the controversy
- 8-10s: stat reveal ("73% of people disagree with you")

**Sample script**:
> "Wait, the game is telling me that Hide The Pain Harold has more aura than Andrew Tate giving life advice? [pause] Actually, yeah. Yeah it does. 89% agree. Andrew Tate has -71,000 aura, by the way. Negative seventy-one thousand. Link in bio."

**Variations**:
1. Harold vs Andrew Tate
2. Dhoni vs Kohli (very controversial in India)
3. Messi vs Ronaldo
4. Walter White vs Tony Soprano
5. Skibidi vs LinkedIn motivational
6. Pineapple pizza vs Spanish omelette with onion

## Format 5 — Weekly recap

**Length**: 30s
**Why it works**: serialized content. Builds anticipation. Reuses player data as content.

**Structure**:
- 0-3s: hook ("Top 5 deaths this week")
- 3-25s: 5 quick cuts of 4 seconds each (death card + sass line)
- 25-30s: final cut, "send yours to be featured"

**Sample script**:
> "Top 5 most controversial Aura Off deaths this week. Number 5: [show card]. Number 4: [show card]. Number 3: this guy thought Skibidi had more aura than John Wick. [show card]. Number 2: [show card]. Number 1: this card is going down in history. [show card]. Send your worst death to be featured next week."

**Variations**:
1. Top 5 deaths this week
2. Top 5 sigma picks this week
3. The 5 most argued-about pairs this week
4. 5 dilemmas that broke the comments
5. Most-shared cards this week
6. Lowest-aura humans according to the algorithm

## Caption strategy

Every caption follows this structure:

```
Hook line in lowercase. (the line that re-states the hook from the video)

(blank line)

Optional question to drive comments. "what's your worst streak?"

(blank line)

#auraoff #fyp #genzindia #indianmemes #brainrot #memesdaily #wholesomememes
+ 2 niche tags relevant to the specific reel
```

Total 10 hashtags. No 30-tag spam.

## Voiceover vs on-screen text

| Format | Voiceover? | On-screen text? |
|---|---|---|
| Bet I can streak | Yes (founder face) | Captions of voiceover |
| POV 3am | Minimal | Most info via text overlay |
| I made X play | Mixed (you + them) | Captions |
| Wait this is more aura | Yes | Captions + stat reveal text |
| Weekly recap | Yes | Death card visible, sass line overlay |

Always add captions. 80% of Reels are watched muted.

## Recording tips

- Phone vertical, mounted, eye level.
- Natural light from a window if possible.
- Record at 4K, edit at 1080p (gives room for stabilization + crop).
- Edit on CapCut (free, fast, mobile).
- Use auto-captions, then manually fix the 2-3 things they get wrong.
- Music: use the platform's native trending sounds. They get amplified. Don't add your own copyrighted music.

## What we don't do in any Reel

- Lip-sync to copyrighted music with our content
- Use video of real people without their consent (especially celebrities — covered in `docs/LEGAL_RISKS.md`)
- Attack named living people in a way that could be defamatory
- Force-feed jargon ("Gen Z this", "millennial that")
- Apologize, hedge, or sound corporate
- Beg for likes ("smash that follow") — modern audiences hate this
- Tag celebrities or brands without intent
