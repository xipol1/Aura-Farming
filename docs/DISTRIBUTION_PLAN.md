# Distribution Plan

## Target market

**Primary**: Gen Z India, urban Tier 1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune). English-fluent. 18–25.
**Secondary**: India diaspora in USA, UK, Canada, Gulf.
**Tertiary**: Gen Z global.

We do not target Hindi-only audiences in v1. Adding Hindi happens only after PMF metrics in `KILL_CRITERIA.md` are exceeded.

---

## Channels (in priority order)

| Channel | Why | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | India's largest short-video platform post TikTok ban (~350M users) | 3 Reels/day cadence, founder-creator content |
| YouTube Shorts | Cross-platform amplification, longer half-life | Cross-post Reels content with platform-specific tweaks |
| WhatsApp | Not discovery, but the *primary share channel* in India | Share card OG-optimized for WhatsApp preview |
| Reddit | r/IndianTeenagers, r/IndianDankMemes, r/india | Native posts with screenshots, no link spam |
| Twitter/X India | Smaller but high-influence Tier 1 + diaspora | Threaded sharing of viral game-overs |
| Moj / Josh | Indian short video, regional reach | Phase 2 if PMF achieved |
| TikTok | Banned in India. Use for global diaspora only | Diaspora audience only |

## 90-day playbook

### Week -2 to 0 (pre-launch)

- Create accounts: `@auraoff` on Instagram, YouTube, Twitter, Reddit. Lock the handle.
- Generate 30 sample share cards from the dev build.
- Record 30 Reels using those cards (founder records, no faces needed — over-the-shoulder phone recording works).
- Set up Linktree or single landing page collecting all links.
- Prepare 50 cold DMs in CREATOR_OUTREACH_TEMPLATE.md, identify recipients.

### Week 1 post-launch

- Day 1: post 3 Reels (9am, 5pm, 9pm IST).
- Day 2-7: same cadence, 21 Reels total.
- Day 3: drop on Reddit r/IndianTeenagers with screenshot post format (see REELS_SCRIPT_TEMPLATES.md).
- Day 5: 25 cold DMs to micro-creators (5-50k followers).
- Day 7: review what gained traction. Double down on the format that worked.

### Week 2-4

- Continue 3 Reels/day.
- Each Friday: launch the "Aura Off Weekly Top 10" — a Reel ranking the funniest deaths submitted by community. This is recurring viral content with zero marginal effort once the format is set.
- Test 2 new Reel formats per week (POV, challenge-a-friend, react-with-shock).
- Cold DM next 25 creators.
- Engage in every comment on every Reel for the first 1 hour. India's algorithm rewards velocity.

### Week 5-8

- If metrics pass kill criteria: contact 2-3 mid-tier creators (50-300k) for collab proposals. Free access, ego flex, possibly co-branding. No paid posts.
- Launch leaderboard feature (top 100 streaks of the week).
- Test running an "Aura Off Tournament" — 24-hour event with the highest streak winning a meme award (zero cash).

### Week 9-12

- Evaluate against KILL_CRITERIA.md.
- If PMF strong: localize to Hindi.
- If PMF moderate: iterate on retention features (themed decks, daily challenges).
- If PMF weak: pivot or kill.

## Content formats that work on Reels India (based on observed trends)

### Format 1: "Bet I can streak X"

```
HOOK (0-2s): "I bet I can streak 50 in this aura game without dying."
BODY (2-12s): Fast cuts of gameplay with reactions.
CLIMAX (12-14s): The moment of death, exaggerated reaction.
CTA (14-15s): "Link in bio. Beat me if you can."
```

### Format 2: "POV: you're playing at 3am"

```
HOOK: "POV: it's 3am and you can't stop playing Aura Off."
BODY: Loop of gameplay with eye bags filter, lo-fi audio.
CLIMAX: Auto-cut on a particularly absurd comparison.
CTA: "Try it. Don't blame me."
```

### Format 3: "I made my [parent/friend/girlfriend] play"

```
HOOK: "I made my [whoever] play Aura Off."
BODY: Their reaction to absurd comparisons, especially the cringe ones.
CLIMAX: Their score vs yours.
CTA: "Tag someone who would lose at this."
```

### Format 4: "Wait, this is more aura than THAT?"

```
HOOK: Screenshot of a controversial pair with mouth-agape filter.
BODY: Founder explains the controversy in 10 seconds.
CLIMAX: The community percentage that disagrees with you.
CTA: "Settle it. Link in bio."
```

### Format 5: Weekly recap

```
HOOK: "Top 5 most controversial Aura Off deaths this week."
BODY: 5 cuts × 2 seconds each, each a death card with sass.
CLIMAX: The most absurd one of the week.
CTA: "What's your worst streak? Comment it."
```

## Posting cadence

- **Instagram Reels**: 3/day. Slots: 9am, 5pm, 9pm IST.
- **YouTube Shorts**: 1-2/day. Repost Reels with descriptive titles + tags.
- **Reddit**: 2/week max. Different subs. Native posts, no cross-posting (Reddit hates it).
- **Twitter**: 1-2 threads/week. Highest-engagement moments only.

Quality > quantity if forced to choose, but in the first 30 days the algorithm rewards consistency over polish. Ship daily.

---

## Comment engagement protocol

When a Reel posts:
- First 60 minutes: respond to every comment.
- First 24 hours: respond to every comment with a question.
- Day 2-7: respond to all questions, like all positive comments.

This signals to the algorithm that the post is "engaging" and boosts further distribution.

- 5 mid-volume: `#auraoff #genzindia #brainrot #memesdaily #wholesomememes`

YouTube Shorts:
- Use the search bar's autocomplete to find what's trending in the moment.
- Always tag `#shorts` for the Shorts surface.

## Creator outreach: cold DM playbook

See `CREATOR_OUTREACH_TEMPLATE.md` for the exact scripts.

Principles:
- **No paid posts in v1.** We're broke and unproven.
- **Offer**: free beta access (it's free anyway), a "featured" mention if they post, custom branded share cards if they go viral.
- **Volume**: 25 DMs/week, expect 5% response rate (5-7 replies), 1-2 actual posts.
- **Target profile**: 5-50k followers, Gen Z India, humor/college/relatable content nichd.
